Give Me Plot Possibilities

Here are three genuinely different directions.

Nothing becomes canon just because it's offered here. Choose one, combine ideas, or ask a question about any of them.

OPTION A
Daniella initially hides the dreams
Why it could work — builds tension from isolation; the reader knows more than the people around her do.
What it would change — her arc becomes about the cost of secrecy, not just discovery.
Possible conflict — she has to actively lie or deflect people who are trying to help her.
Character consequences — risks reading as evasive rather than afraid, unless her reasons stay clear.
Questions it creates — who eventually finds out, and how?
OPTION B
Daniella tells someone immediately but isn't believed
Why it could work — external conflict from the very first dream, instead of waiting for one to build.
What it would change — the early chapters carry more relational friction, less internal rumination.
Possible conflict — whoever doubts her becomes a recurring pressure, not a one-scene moment.
Character consequences — she has to decide whether to keep insisting or go quiet — a second choice, not just one.
Questions it creates — what finally makes that person believe her, and when?
OPTION C
Someone else recognizes what's happening before Daniella does
Why it could work — lets a mentor or elder character carry early spiritual authority, and gives Daniella someone to grow toward.
What it would change — Daniella's arc becomes catch-up and ownership rather than discovery from zero.
Possible conflict — she may resist being told what she's experiencing before she's ready to own it herself.
Character consequences — risks sidelining her agency in her own story if that character explains too much, too soon.
Questions it creates — how much does that person already know, and why haven't they said anything until now?
Combine ideas None of these — reject all

Chapter 21 — draft outline

Purpose
Daniella goes to the prophetic meeting with Selah and has a real encounter — the reader should feel her gift growing, not just hear about it.
Opening state
Daniella is nervous but resolved — she's already told God she'll go, and she means it.
Scene 1 — Daniella and Selah, arriving
Goal: Daniella wants to walk in without letting her nerves show, for Selah's sake as much as her own.
Setting: The converted room off the fellowship hall — see Setting Builder.
  • Daniella and Selah find seats; the room is smaller and plainer than either expected.
  • Someone is already praying quietly over another woman in the corner.
  • Selah leans over and asks, quietly, if this is normal.
Scene 2 — the encounter
Goal: Daniella wants to actually hear something — not perform, just receive.
  • The leader invites people to share what they're sensing.
  • Daniella hesitates, then shares a picture she's been carrying since the drive over.
  • It lands — someone in the room confirms it means something to them.
Chapter end state
Daniella: Steadier. Less afraid this is her imagination. Selah: Curious, a little unsettled, wants to talk about it on the drive home. Relationship: unchanged, but Selah has now seen this side of Daniella firsthand.
New questions created

Continuity notes
